# Family Feud Official TV Rules (sourced from familyfeudlive.com, Wikipedia, wiki)

## TEAMS
- 5 players per team (our app allows flexible sizes)
- Each team has a play order (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th)

## FACE-OFF
- Player in position N from each team face off in Round N
- Host reads the question; both players buzz in
- First to buzz gives their answer first
- If answer is on the board, the other player then gives their answer
- Whichever answer ranks HIGHER wins the face-off
- If only one answer is on the board, that player wins automatically
- If NEITHER answer is on the board, the next pair (position 2) face off
- Winner chooses PLAY or PASS
- 3-second response window after buzzing

## MAIN ROUND (PLAY)
- Controlling team answers in strict sequential turn order
- Each player gives ONE answer per turn
- 5-second response window
- If answer is on the board: tile flips, points accumulate
- If answer is NOT on the board: STRIKE
- If answer is a DUPLICATE of an already-revealed answer: STRIKE (counts as wrong)
- After 3 strikes: team loses control, STEAL opportunity
- If team reveals ALL answers before 3 strikes: they win all points
- Points are NOT awarded during play — they're banked at round end

## STEAL
- Opposing team gets ONE chance to steal
- Team CAN huddle/discuss before answering
- One designated player gives the final answer
- If correct (matches ANY unrevealed answer): stealing team gets ALL accumulated points
- If incorrect: controlling team keeps ALL accumulated points
- Only need to match ONE remaining answer to steal

## SCORING / ROUNDS
- Round 1: 1x multiplier
- Round 2: 1x multiplier
- Round 3: 2x multiplier (every answer worth double)
- Round 4: 3x multiplier (every answer worth triple)
- Points awarded at END of each round (after play or steal resolves)
- First to 300 points wins (current format)
- If neither team reaches 300 after 4 rounds: sudden death triple-value face-off
- If a team reaches 300 mid-game, game ends immediately

## FAST MONEY
- Winning team selects 2 players
- Player 2 is SEQUESTERED (cannot hear/see Player 1)
- Player 1: 20 seconds, 5 questions rapid-fire
- Player 2: 25 seconds, same 5 questions
- If Player 2 gives same answer as Player 1: "Try again!" (not a strike, just costs time)
- Combined total of 200+ points wins the grand prize
- Reveal: question by question, Player 1 answer + points, then Player 2 answer + points
- Running total shown, celebration at 200+
